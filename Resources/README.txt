------- -------------
* Locate SETUP.exe and run it, UAC Prompt might occur press yes.

* After Installation Open c:\Car_Rental_Management_System and locate CRMS_MAIN.exe.
* Right click and create a shortcut on desktop for ease of access.
* Continue to enjoy the program.
